# Sentinel: The Sentience Layer for AI Infrastructure

## The Core Mission
In the gold rush of the Generative AI era, infrastructure has become the most precious commodity. Yet, most H100 clusters and high-density liquid-cooled racks remain "Black Boxes"—silent until they fail, and inefficient by default.

**Sentinel** is designed to be the universal "Sentience Layer" for this new era. It is a high-performance, polyglot, and autonomous observability engine that transforms raw hardware signals into actionable intelligence.

---

## The Three Pillars of the Sentinel Vision

### 1. Infinite Extensibility (The Skill Architecture)
We believe that infrastructure diversity is an asset, not a burden. Sentinel's **WASM-powered Skill System** allows anyone to write a monitor for a proprietary liquid cooling loop, a 20-year-old PDU, or a custom FPGA in **Python, JavaScript, Rust, or Go**.
- **The Goal**: A community-driven "Skill Store" where any piece of hardware can be integrated in minutes, not months.

### 2. Autonomic Efficiency (Beyond Dashboards)
Monitoring is passive; **Sentience is active**. Sentinel doesn't just show you a graph of a GPU thermal throttling—it detects the root cause (e.g., PCIe degradation or fan failure) and triggers immediate mitigation via the Skill System.
- **The Goal**: Zero-touch infrastructure. Sentinel handles the "preflight" and "healing" so researchers can focus on the models.

### 3. Energy-Aware Orchestration (The Green AI Standard)
Energy is the ultimate bottleneck for AI. Sentinel treats **Power and PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness)** as first-class citizens, correlating token throughput with Joule consumption in real-time.
- **The Goal**: To become the global "Efficiency Score" for AI Datacenters, enabling a future where AI scales sustainably.

---

## Momentum & Mass Adoption Strategy

To achieve universal adoption, Sentinel follows the **"Infrastructure as an Experience"** philosophy:

1.  **Zero-Friction Entry**: A single binary, a single Docker command, or a single Helm chart. No complex dependencies.
2.  **The "Wow" Factor**: A premium Terminal User Interface (TUI) that makes infrastructure command and control feel like a first-class developer experience.
3.  **Polyglot First**: By supporting Python and JavaScript for WASM skills, we open the platform to millions of developers who are not systems engineers but need infrastructure visibility.
4.  **10ms Telemetry**: In a world of 50ms GPU frequency bursts, 1-minute scrapes are useless. Sentinel is built for "High-Frequency Sentience."

---

## Roadmap to the "Sentience Standard"

- **Phase 1: The Observability Engine** (Current) - High-frequency telemetry for GPU, CPU, and Power.
- **Phase 2: The Skill Ecosystem** - A vibrant community gallery of WASM-based hardware connectors.
- **Phase 3: The Autonomic Layer** - Local Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and predictive failure scoring (0-100).
- **Phase 4: The Efficiency Protocol** - Standardizing performance-per-watt metrics across the entire AI stack.

*"Sentinel is the BIOS for the AI Datacenter. It is the layer that makes the machines speak a language we can finally act upon."*
